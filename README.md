Breadcrumbs is built to make digital overload less overwhelming. With our extension that automatically sorts your bookmarks for you and adds them to your bookmarks bar, we plan to erase much of the disarray that lives on your bookmarks bar. Our extension helps you define exactly what each bookmark is for, making you more aware of the links that you save to make sure you come back to them. Our joint Breadcrumbs app, which syncs to the data on your extension, is designed to inspire digital self-awareness and absorb the knowledge you might glance at and never look at again otherwise. Breadcrumbs helps you retain that information for the long term with scheduled spaced repetition, focus sessions, and AI-generated podcasts. With analytics on your past week and month, Breadcrumbs gives you actionable steps to complete and protects you from burnout by structuring your goals to make them achievable. Whether you’re balancing heavy academic coursework or personal research, Breadcrumbs empowers you to optimize your learning, master your time, and stay in rhythm with your goals.  

**Why Did We Create This?**
I felt inspired to create this app because of the amount of digital clutter on my bookmarks bar. I used to save around three links per day for things I thought were interesting, things I wanted to look into, or things to help me with school. Over time, my bookmarks became a long list on my bar, and I felt unmotivated and unproductive imagining the long list of things I had to go through. I looked at the browsers of my friends and family and realized they had a lot of meaningless bookmarks and clutter that overwhelmed them when they sat down to accomplish something. I created Breadcrumbs, initially, to just sort my bookmarks and make my tasks look more manageable.

However, it soon became so much more as I saw how friends around me were struggling to see how much time they actually dedicated to each of their core subjects and passions. They constantly complained of putting time into a subject and not getting results, which made me realize that there was no clear-cut way for them to track how the time they were investing actually resulted in progress. This led to the creation of the goal velocity section, which tells you how much of the time you're investing is actually resulting in measurable progress.

Additionally, so many of us don't know how to adjust our time properly to accommodate new priorities. I saw this in myself: whenever I had an upcoming math competition, I would throw everything else to the side and begin preparation. This often led to a backlog of schoolwork, late due dates, and less time for the things I enjoyed, like reading and casual browsing. Our interactive sandbox feature is meant to predict how much time you would lose on each of your passions if you put that time elsewhere.

Too many of us have issues visualizing how our time shifts throughout the week and how we're meant to be flexible with how we manage it. I observed this in my classmates, with many complaining that they wasted the entire day scrolling because they just weren't able to see the time visually passing by. This inspired me to create our visual planner, which allows people to drag and drop activities into time slots and see, visually, how much time they're allocating for rest and relaxation—and how much time they really have in a day.  

**AI USAGE IN THE APP**
Throughout learning how to code, AI tools—such as GitHub Copilot in VS Code—have been a great resource for us to learn how to accomplish specific tasks without the hassle of searching through long books. As coding the extension became harder, AI became a vital tool we used to learn how to implement certain features. Whenever we did not know how to go about coding something, AI helped guide and teach us the correct way to code it successfully.

In the extension itself, all ideas and features were created entirely by Aadya and Lily. We spent weeks planning what we wanted each page in the extension to do and figuring out how the app should function. AI helped us fully execute these ideas through step-by-step guidance, adapting to our knowledge level since we did not have an actual mentor to guide us. For example, Lily and Aadya initially didn't know how to go about automatically sorting bookmarks or what permissions were required to automatically add them to the browser bookmarks bar, so we used AI to walk us through, step by step, the specific permissions we needed to enable.

Furthermore, we used AI when syncing our Appwrite database to the actual data being displayed in the app. Because we didn't have much experience with backend databases or advanced coding, whenever we needed to troubleshoot bookmarks not popping up or graphs not displaying properly, we used AI to identify what pieces of data were being lost in transit.

Beyond development, AI is also a core part of the functionality of our app—powering our AI podcasts, AI-generated quizzes, activity summaries, AI chatbots, goal analytics, and personalized data insights. These features improve overall personalization, making users feel like the app truly knows them and understands their patterns. Our integration of AI throughout both development and app design directly fulfills this purpose.
